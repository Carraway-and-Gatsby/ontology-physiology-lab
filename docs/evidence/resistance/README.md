# Resistance-path evidence (ONTO-RES-02, 2026-11-02)

`approved_notes.json` retains findings section 3 wording verbatim. `terms.json`
is a bounded projection of 13 exact OLS term records (IDs, labels, IRIs, FMA
xrefs, obsolescence status). `sources.json` identifies original response hashes
and retained excerpt files. Full literature bodies are deliberately not included.

All four literature sources have unconfirmed redistribution licences. Excerpts
are bounded; source-response hashes do not hash the reduced files. Two excerpt
annotations are corrected in the ledger: no anesthesia assumption from the hamster
abstract, and the pulmonary pressure-drop quotation is in Figure 2's legend.

No exact small/large artery identifier was established in this evidence; small
arteries stay a local segment. Muscular artery is retained as an evidenced term,
without asserting it is equivalent to small artery. Generic arteriole, capillary,
and venule are systemic only by path context. FMA entries are source xrefs, not
new identity claims. No FMA xref is supplied for pulmonary venule. OLS reports
pulmonary arteriole and venule non-obsolete in the defining ontology; future
identifier stability and ontology-level licences have not been independently verified.

Percentages remain source-attributed note text, never model numbers. Systemic
pressure-drop measurements require equal-flow series assumptions to infer a
resistance share and do not establish whole-human parallel-bed fractions. Pulmonary
numbers refer to isolated dog lobes or preparation-dependent review pressure drops;
the denominators differ, the review numbers sum to 86%, and its arterial figures
are internally inconsistent. No general human in-vivo split is established.
