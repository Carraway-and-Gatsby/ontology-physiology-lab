## 2. Other semantic or annotation-based composition and validation work

- OMEX Metadata v1.2, Gennari et al. 2021, J Integr Bioinform 18(3), PMID 34668356, doi:10.1515/jib-2021-0020 (A): encoding guideline; no connection-legality semantics. SHACL shapes for v1.2 not found in the literature (negative, unconfirmed).
- COMBINE archive, Bergmann et al. 2014, BMC Bioinformatics 15:369, PMID 25494900 (A): container only.
- CellML 2.0, Clerx et al. 2020, J Integr Bioinform 17(2-3), PMID 32759406 (A). libCellML: PyPI 0.7.2, Apache-2.0, CellML 2.0 only; Validator and Analyser catch unit errors after flattening (S, tutorials). State-of-the-art units-only baseline.
- SBML Level 3 comp, Smith et al. 2015, J Integr Bioinform 12(2):268, PMID 26528566 (A): ports optional; structural validation only.
- semanticSBML, Krause et al. 2010, Bioinformatics 26:421-2, PMID 19933161 (A): MIRIAM-annotation element matching; no error-rate evaluation.
- CelOWS, Matos et al. 2009, J Biomed Inform 43:125-36, PMID 19695346 (A): OWL-DL ontology to "describe, query and compose CellML models" with "semantic validation"; oldest direct precedent; no quantitative evaluation.
- PMR annotation and discovery, Sarwar et al. 2019, BMC Bioinformatics 20:457, PMID 31492098 (A; workspace 267 page W): workspace 267 is a demo workspace (owner Dewan Sarwar) holding Smith_2004.cellml among 43 models.
- BioSimulators, Shaikh et al. 2022, Nucleic Acids Res 50:W108-14, PMID 35524558 (A): referential-integrity validation only.
- Physiome journal (W): reproducibility curation tied to a primary paper; not composition validation.
- Bond-graph composition: Gawthrop and Crampin 2014, Proc R Soc A 470:20140459, PMID 25383030 (A); Pan et al. 2018, Proc R Soc A 474:20180106, PMID 29977132 (A); Cudmore et al. 2021, Eur Phys J E 44:148, PMID 34904197 (A); Gawthrop, Pan, Crampin 2021, J R Soc Interface 18:20210478, PMID 34428949 (A). **Shahidi et al. 2021, PLoS Comput Biol 17:e1008859, PMID 33983945, doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008859 (FT)**: bond-graph CellML templates plus SemGen merging rebuild the 86-segment ADAN arterial model, validated by NRMSE; "Existing merging tools like SemGen do not consider physical constraints, while the bond graph framework imposes these constraints". Shahidi et al. 2022, PLoS One 17:e0269497, PMID 35657966 (A) and Math Biosci 352:108901, PMID 36096376 (A): automatic SBML to bond graph and semantic port matching. Unresolved: semantic mis-wiring with correct energy domain; no error-injection study; requires re-encoding legacy models.
- Unit checking outside biology (S): Modelica dimensional checking still being standardised; Pint raises DimensionalityError at runtime.
- 2023 to 2026: Talk2Biomodels (Wehling et al. 2025, BMC Bioinformatics 26:276, PMID 41254502, A) is query and simulation, not composition; SBMLLM-Bench (S) benchmarks LLM reconstruction of SBML; "From FAIR to CURE" (Sauro et al., npj Syst Biol Appl 2026, doi:10.1038/s41540-026-00651-0, S) is a citable motivation. No paper on ontology-based validation of port connections between physiological models was found.

## 3. Track B models

**Smith BW, Chase JG, Nokes RI, Shaw GM, Wake G. Med Eng Phys 2004;26(2):131-9, PMID 15036180, doi:10.1016/j.medengphy.2003.10.001** (A plus CellML W). Six volume states, septum volume via algebraic constraint, pericardium, thoracic pressure, four inertial valve flows with "open on pressure close on flow", no atria. Abstract results: 9 percent CO drop raising P_th from -4 to 0 mmHg; 120/80 to 165/130 on doubling R_sys. PMR exposure 9d046663ba5cac5c8a61ac146183614b: CellML 1.0, 14 components, creator Geoffrey Nunns, changeset f7b732c9707abdba5a55d471c49baf7dee92b036; runs in PCEnv and OpenCell; "will not run in COR because it contains differential algebraic equations"; translated from the authors' Matlab; 14 beats encoded; CC BY 3.0; no SED-ML, no reference plots. Follow-ups (A): Hann 2005 CMPB 80:65-74, PMID 16039750; Hann 2006 CMPB 81:181-92, PMID 16413632; Smith 2006 Physiol Meas 27:165-79, PMID 16400203; Starfinger 2007 CMPB 87:46-60, PMID 17478006; Starfinger 2008 CMPB 91:128-34 and 135-44, PMID 18472180 and 18466998; Starfinger 2008 Math Biosci 216:132-9, PMID 18817788; Paeme 2011 Biomed Eng Online 10:86, PMID 21942971 (CC BY; Tables 1 to 3 give most parameters, Aorta Ees printed 0.0, R_sys and R_pul and initial conditions omitted); Revie 2013, PMID 23585774; Pironet 2013 PLoS One 8:e65146, PMID 23755183 (adds a left atrium; MATLAB File S1 CC BY, but a dog sarcomere model set); Pironet 2016 Med Eng Phys 38:433-41, PMID 26970891; de Bournonville 2019 Math Biosci 313:81-94, PMID 31128126. Open questions a hybrid could still address (synthesis, not claimed by any source): atrial-kick contribution to LV filling under imposed septal shift; regurgitant-closure volume and E/A changes under PEEP-driven thoracic pressure with septal displacement; whether annulus or AV-plane motion modifies the direct-versus-series interaction decomposition of Smith 2006. Any discovery must come from a specific data-confronted prediction, not from the combination per se.

## 6. Journal criteria (quoted where obtained)

- Nature Computational Science aims (W): "publishes both fundamental and applied research, from groundbreaking algorithms, tools and frameworks that notably help to advance scientific research, to methodologies that use computing capabilities in novel ways". Editorial policy (via text proxy): authors of research articles "that contain newly developed code that is central to the main claims" must supply it for review; Nature Portfolio requires a Code Availability section and a data availability statement.
- Nature Communications aims (W): "Papers published by the journal aim to represent important advances of significance to specialists within each field." Peer-review page: "an advance in understanding likely to influence thinking in the field" with "strong evidence for their conclusions".
- Specialist alternatives, Track A: PLoS Comput Biol (methods must be "of exceptional importance" with "potential for being widely adopted"); Bioinformatics ("New methods MUST be compared to existing state-of-the-art methods, using real biological data"); Briefings in Bioinformatics; J Integr Bioinform (home of COMBINE specifications); Physiome.
- Specialist alternatives, Track B: AJP-Heart Circ Physiol ("significant new mechanistic physiological insights"); Frontiers in Physiology, Computational Physiology and Medicine; Med Eng Phys; J R Soc Interface; Comput Biol Med.

## 7. Novelty verdict

**Track A.** A quantified, controlled fault-injection benchmark with units-only and accept-all baselines is not in the literature. Non-novel if claimed: "first ontology-based composition" (SemGen, semanticSBML, CelOWS, Shahidi), unit-mismatch rejection as the contribution (libCellML, Modelica, Pint), or a 12-fixture synthetic benchmark alone (fails the "real biological data" rule). Credible contribution requires a formal rule set grounded in OPB and RO, a benchmark mixing synthetic faults with faults mined from real merges, comparison against SemGen exact-match and libCellML units validation, and ideally one real integration where the validator catches a physiology-changing mistake. Nature Computational Science is a stretch unless the method generalises beyond CellML and cardiovascular models.

**Track B.** The specific Smith × Korakianitis–Shi hybrid is not in the literature, but the ingredient combination is (Mynard and Smolich 2015, Broomé 2013). A Nature Communications-level result would need a mechanistic prediction confirmed against data (the Christchurch and Liège porcine PEEP and embolism datasets are the obvious targets) and shown insensitive to the valve-model choice. Without data confrontation the realistic venues are Med Eng Phys, Frontiers in Physiology or AJP-Heart.
