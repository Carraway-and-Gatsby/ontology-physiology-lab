# Third-party notices

Retained third-party material keeps its upstream licence and copyright notices.
This file records attribution and the offline audit findings; it does not relicense
third-party material. Original licence texts and source ledgers remain in `docs/`.
See `RIGHTS.md` for omitted captures, redacted abstracts, and original-byte hashes.

## Smith 2004 CellML encodings

`docs/evidence/a_smith_2004.cellml` and
`docs/evidence/a_smith_2004_canonical.cellml` are retained as complete encodings
under the binding integrator decision that PMR distributes the whole models under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), including embedded
model documentation. These files are unchanged by the rights transform.

Attribution: Smith BW, Chase JG, Nokes RI, Shaw GM, Wake G, *Medical Engineering
& Physics* 26 (2004), 131–139, PMID 15036180. Canonical CellML encoding by
Geoffrey Nunns, Auckland Bioengineering Institute. PMR: Lloyd CM, Lawson JL,
Hunter PJ, Nielsen PF, *Bioinformatics* 2008;24(18):2122–2123.

Sources: [PMR exposure and licence/citation](https://models.physiomeproject.org/exposure/9d046663ba5cac5c8a61ac146183614b/smith_chase_nokes_shaw_wake_2004.cellml/license_citation)
and [SemSim-annotated workspace 267 encoding](https://models.physiomeproject.org/workspace/267/rawfile/6ae217019bf59fc1e22e2dc5235751c6fcf6be24/Smith_2004.cellml).
The captured licence statements and model attribution are retained in
`docs/evidence/a_smith_2004_license.txt` and `docs/physiology_sources.json`.
